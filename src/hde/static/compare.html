SERIAL DEPTH / WHAT THINKING BUYS

Sixteen arrows, and a walk of k steps.

A table of sixteen numbers, each pointing at one other, is written into the prompt. A question asks where a walk of k arrows ends. Every arrow is on the page, so nothing has to be known that is not there; what the question demands is that the j-th lookup wait for the (j−1)-th, because which row to read next is the answer to the row before. That makes k a dial on serial depth with everything else held still — the candidate set is the same sixteen numbers at every depth, so chance does not move either.
